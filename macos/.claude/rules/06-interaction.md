# macOS interaction

- Important commands have menu-bar access, with shortcuts that follow macOS conventions.
- Preserve standard text editing shortcuts and focus behavior.
- Support contextual actions where they help.
- Use sheets, alerts and dialogs appropriately, and avoid needless modal interruption.
- Mark destructive actions clearly and provide undo for reversible operations.
- Do not rely on hover alone. Every action works with both keyboard and pointer.
