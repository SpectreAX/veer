# Apple HIG

The official guidelines are the source of truth. Links are in `Design/HIG_REFERENCES.md`.

- Design for Mac workflows, not enlarged mobile ones.
- Use familiar window, navigation, menu and selection behavior.
- Prefer standard controls and avoid decoration that carries no meaning.
- Do not invent an interaction pattern without a reason.
- Cite a real HIG page for a claimed violation. Never invent an Apple requirement.
- A deviation listed in the project's design spec is not a finding unless its reason no longer holds.
  A new deviation goes in that table with its reason, or it gets fixed.

Classify findings:

- **Violation:** demonstrably conflicts with platform guidance.
- **Risk:** likely to confuse or reduce usability.
- **Preference:** a matter of taste. Never call this a HIG violation.

Only confirmed violations block a task.
